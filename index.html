FSM Clipper

FSM Clipper is the personal desktop tool behind the FunnyStreamMoment VN channel. It turns funny moments from livestreams into short vertical clips, which the owner reviews and then publishes to their own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram.

How it works

  • The app runs locally on the owner's computer; it is not a public service and has no user sign-up.
  • It finds moments in a livestream, cuts a short clip, adds subtitles and credits the original streamer.
  • Every clip is reviewed by the owner before it is published. Nothing is posted automatically.
  • With TikTok, the clip is uploaded to the owner's TikTok inbox (Content Posting API); the owner finishes and posts it in the TikTok app.
