Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Who uses the app

FSM Clipper is a personal desktop tool used only by the owner of the FunnyStreamMoment VN channel. It has no public users, no sign-up and no analytics or advertising trackers.

2. Data the app accesses

  • TikTok: with the owner's permission (Login Kit, scope user.info.basic), the app reads the owner's TikTok display name to show which account is connected. With video.upload (Content Posting API) it uploads clips the owner has approved to the owner's TikTok inbox.
  • YouTube, Facebook, Instagram: with the owner's permission, the app uploads approved clips to the owner's own channel, Page or account and reads the channel or account name.
  • Access tokens: stored only on the owner's computer, in a local folder that is never shared or uploaded.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not post anything without the owner's review.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account in the app, which deletes them immediately. Access can also be revoked at any time in the settings of each platform (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).
